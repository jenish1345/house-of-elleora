Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

House of Elleora ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect when you shop with us and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your delivery address
  • The products you order

Wishlist items and reviews you write are saved only in your own browser unless you send them to us.

Why we collect it

  • To process, ship and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order (WhatsApp, phone or email)

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone. We share it only with delivery partners, and only what they need to deliver your order.

How we protect it

Your order details are stored in a secured database that only our admin can access. The website uses HTTPS encryption.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as needed for delivery, returns and our accounting records, and then delete them.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we can.
